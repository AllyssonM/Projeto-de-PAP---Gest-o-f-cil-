## Third-party services and sub-processors

{{nome}} uses the services below **only when the relevant feature is used**. The list is kept up to date; the date of the latest revision is shown in each document.

| Service | What it is for | Data involved | When it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hosting and database | Storing the system and all the data | All the data described in the Privacy Policy | Always. The location depends on who installs {{nome}}; a server in the European Union is recommended. |
| Email server (SMTP) | Sending account emails (password recovery, email confirmation, invitations) | Email address and email content | When there are emails to send |
| Open-Meteo | Weather | City or coordinates requested and IP address | Only if you use the weather feature |
| Google Calendar | Syncing your calendar | Events from the calendars you authorise | Only if you connect Google |
| AI provider | Natural-language assistant | Your questions and the data strictly necessary to answer them | Only if Lumina has been enabled by the administrator |

Some of these services may process data outside the European Economic Area; in those cases, appropriate safeguards must be in place (for example, standard contractual clauses).

## Your rights and who supervises them

You can **download all your data** and **delete your account** in the Personal area ("Security and session" tab). More details in the [Privacy Policy](politica-privacidade).

The supervisory authority in Portugal is the [Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)](https://www.cnpd.pt), the Portuguese data protection authority.{{livro_frase}}
