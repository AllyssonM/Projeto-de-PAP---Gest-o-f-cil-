# Cookie Policy

Descrição: Everything Lumina stores in your browser: essential storage only, no advertising or analytics cookies. Full list and how to delete it.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. There is also "local storage", which serves the same purpose (remembering choices). On this page we treat both in the same way.

## What {{nome}} stores in your browser

{{nome}} uses **essential storage only**, needed for the system to work or to remember choices you make. **We do not use advertising cookies, profiling cookies or visit analytics cookies.**

Tabela: Cookies and local storage used by Lumina
| Name | What it is for | Duration | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PHPSESSID` | Keeps you signed in and protects requests against forgery. It is only sent to our server. | Until you close the browser | Essential (cookie) |
| `gf-tema` | Remembers the light or dark theme. | Until you delete it | Preference |
| `gf-tab` | Remembers the tab you were on in the dashboard. | Until you close the tab | Essential (session) |
| `gf-hide-values` | Remembers whether you hid the financial values (the preference is also saved in your account). | Until you delete it | Preference |
| `lumina-chart-overview`, `lumina-chart-report` | Remember the period and the type (bars or lines) of the charts. | Until you delete them | Preference |
| `gf-note-draft` | Saves a draft of a note that has not been saved yet, so you do not lose it. | Until the note is saved | Essential |
| `gf-cidade` | Remembers the weather city. | Until you delete it | Preference |
| `gf-vehicle` | Remembers the last vehicle used when recording trips. | Until you delete it | Preference |
| `gf-reptile` | Remembers whether you turned a decorative visual effect on or off. | Until you delete it | Preference |
| `lumina-aviso-cookies` | Remembers that you have seen the cookie notice. | Until you delete it | Essential |
| Browser cache (`lumina-static-v1`) | Stores site files (appearance and code, never your data) so the site opens faster and so you can install Lumina on your phone. | Until a new version or until you delete it | Essential |

## Third-party services

- **Open-Meteo** (weather): only when you use this feature, your browser requests the forecast from this service, which receives the city or coordinates and your IP address. We do not set cookies through it.
- **Google**: only if you connect Google Calendar. Authorisation is given on Google's website, which has its own cookie rules.

By default, the home page does not load third-party content.

## Consent

Strictly necessary storage does not need your consent. We show an informational notice on your first visit; just click "Got it". If we ever start using non-essential cookies (for example, analytics), we will ask for your consent *beforehand*.

## How to manage or delete

You can view and delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings (usually under "Privacy and security" > "Site data"). If you delete the session cookie, you will have to sign in again; preferences return to their defaults.

For more information on how we process personal data, see the [Privacy Policy](politica-privacidade).
