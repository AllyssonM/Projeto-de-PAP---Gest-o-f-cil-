# Privacy Policy

Descrição: What data Lumina processes, why, for how long and who it is shared with. How to exercise your GDPR (RGPD) rights, including downloading and deleting everything.

## 1. Who is responsible

The controller of the personal data described in this policy is **{{responsavel}}**{{nif_linha}}{{morada_linha}}.

For any request about your data, contact us {{contacto_privacidade}}.

When you use {{nome}} to process data of *your* customers, employees or suppliers, you are the controller of that data and we process it on your behalf, only to provide the service to you.

## 2. What data we process

  - **Account:** name, email, phone (optional), role, profile photo (optional) and your password, which we store *encrypted* (never in readable text).
  - **Company:** name, activity, tax number (NIF), address, phone, business email, website, logo and main colour (all optional, except the name).
  - **What you record in the system:** cash movements, accounts payable and receivable, customers, products (properties, vehicles, services, trips... depending on the line of business), sales, trips, notes, calendar events and working time records.
  - **Team:** when the administrator creates employees, we process their name, email, job title, permissions and recorded schedules.
  - **Team monitoring:** so that the administrator can organise the work, Lumina records and shows them, for each employee: the **status** (online, away, on a break or offline, calculated from activity in the application itself; the break is set by the employee), the **system logins**, the **shifts** (clock-in and clock-out), the **sales** the employee records, the **tasks and goals** assigned to them, the administrator's **notes** (private or shared with the employee) and the **messages** exchanged between the two. Lumina does **not** see your screen, does **not** record what you type outside the application and does **not** use your location for this. Each employee sees, in their own area, what the administrator can see.
  - **Attachments (receipts and invoices):** the photos and PDFs you add to movements, accounts, customers or products. We delete the metadata (for example, GPS location) from photos when saving.
  - **Security:** sessions started (date, IP address and device type), failed login attempts (email and IP), a log of sensitive actions (for example, changing the password) and, if you enable the second step, the authenticator secret (encrypted) and recovery codes (only the encrypted hash).
  - **Linked cards (demonstration):** only the brand, the *last four digits*, the expiry date and the cardholder's name. We **never** store the full number or the CVV.
  - **Lumina, the AI assistant (only if enabled):** your questions and the business data needed to answer them are sent to the configured AI provider. We keep the conversation history and a log of the queries made.
  - **Google Calendar (only if you connect it):** the events from the calendars you choose and the access tokens (encrypted). We never see or store your Google password.
  - **Weather (only if you use the feature):** the city you search for, or the approximate location of your browser if you click "Use my location" and give permission.

## 3. What we use the data for and on what basis

  - **Providing the service** (creating your account, showing your dashboard, storing what you record, managing the team): performance of the contract.
  - **Monitoring the team's work** (status, tasks, goals, messages and notices): the administrator's legitimate interest in organising the work, with transparent information given to employees. It is not used for automated decisions.
  - **Maintaining security** (limiting login attempts, detecting improper access, logging sensitive actions): legitimate interest in protecting the accounts and the system.
  - **Optional features** (location, Google Calendar, Lumina, the AI assistant): your consent or your activation of the feature. You can withdraw it whenever you wish.
  - **Complying with legal obligations**, where applicable.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We do not make automated decisions with legal effects on you.

## 4. Who we share it with

  - **Hosting and database:** the server where {{nome}} is installed processes the data on behalf of the controller.
  - **Open-Meteo** (weather), only when you use that feature: receives the city or coordinates requested and your IP address.
  - **AI provider**, only if Lumina is enabled: receives the questions and the data strictly necessary to answer.
  - **Google**, only if you connect Google Calendar: exchanges with us the events you have authorised.

Some of these services may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA); in such cases, appropriate safeguards must be in place (for example, standard contractual clauses). We do not share data with third parties for any other purpose.

## 5. How long we keep it

  - Account and business data are kept for as long as the account exists. When the account is deleted, the data is erased.
  - Each session started is valid for 14 days and appears under "Active sessions" in the Personal area. The record of a session that has already ended is deleted 30 days later.
  - Failed login attempts are cleared automatically after about 1 day.
  - Links sent by email (password recovery, email confirmation, invitations) are valid for between 1 hour and 3 days, work only once and are deleted 7 days after they expire.
  - The log of Lumina (AI assistant) queries is cleared after 180 days.
  - The log of sensitive actions is kept for as long as the account exists.
  - Employees' **system logins** are kept for 90 days and then deleted.
  - **Notices** (the bell) are deleted 60 days after being read, and all of them after 180 days.
  - The **messages, tasks, goals and notes** of team monitoring are kept for as long as the account exists. If an employee is deleted, their data in this monitoring is deleted along with them.
  - **Attachments** are kept for as long as the movement, account, customer or product they belong to exists; when the record is deleted, the files are deleted too.

## 6. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  - **access** your data and to a copy of it;
  - **rectification** of incorrect data (you can edit your profile and your company in the Personal area);
  - **erasure** ("right to be forgotten");
  - **restriction** of processing and to **object** to it;
  - **portability** of the data you provided to us;
  - **withdraw consent** at any time (for example, switching off Google Calendar or no longer using location).

You can remove the photo and the logo, end sessions, enable the second step and delete notes and records directly in the system. For requests for a copy of your data or complete deletion of the account, contact us through the means indicated in section 1; we reply within the legal deadline (up to one month).

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Portuguese supervisory authority, the [Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), the Portuguese data protection authority](https://www.cnpd.pt).

## 7. How we protect the data

  - Passwords are stored encrypted and login has a limit on attempts.
  - You can enable two-step authentication and view and end open sessions.
  - Second-step secrets and Google tokens are stored encrypted.
  - Each business only sees its own data; each employee only sees the areas the administrator gives them.
  - Actions that change data require a security check of the request (protection against forged requests).

No system is completely invulnerable; if we become aware of a data breach that affects you, we will inform you as required by law.
